# Public asset provenance

The real laboratory portrait and four published research projects come from the portfolio supplied by Mahsa Asghari. Native publication figures retain their original scientific content, axes, legends and scale bars. Original JPEG/PNG extraction files are used without scientific image recoloring or generative reconstruction.

| Public project | Reference |
| --- | --- |
| Speckle dynamics and differential dynamic matrix | https://doi.org/10.1364/OL.564582 |
| Secondary-speckle battery evaluation | https://doi.org/10.1016/j.measurement.2024.116430 |
| Powder dehumidification | https://doi.org/10.1038/s41598-026-51228-0 |
| Remote current sensing in insulated wires | https://doi.org/10.1016/j.measurement.2023.113263 |

The portrait is a reviewed crop of the real laboratory photograph. A smaller JPEG derivative is used for the public PDF. The share card uses this same photograph with exact typeset text.

The asset manifest lists only the public portrait and published figures. Empty one-pixel files at legacy unpublished-image paths exist solely to overwrite previous public assets during a full-package upload; they contain no research images or metadata from that work.

The font subsets derive from Noto Sans and Noto Serif Display. Their binary derivative family names are Research Portfolio Sans and Research Portfolio Serif; the included NOTO_FONT_LICENSE.txt retains the source credit and license terms.

The optical orbit, wavefront sphere, interference rings, speckle field, favicon and interface icons are decorative graphics created in code for the website. They do not represent measured scientific data.

Underlying rights to the supplied portrait and publication figures remain with their respective holders. No blanket license for republishing those assets is granted by this package.
